- [Survival Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit): crate, crate_large, barrel, chest, bucket, signpost, fence_post
- [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit): tree, tree_pine, fence, rock, log, stump, bush
